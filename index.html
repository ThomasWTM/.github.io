关于我About Me

现为北京理工大学博士后研究人员，合作导师为刘驰教授。于大连理工大学获得软件工程博士学位，博士期间曾在北京大学进行交流。

主要研究方向包括水下无线光通信、无线感知与无线充电传感器网络。相关成果发表于 MobiCom、UbiComp、ICNP、IEEE TMC 等 CCF-A/B 类会议与期刊，曾获 IEEE PerCom 2025 最佳演示奖（Best Demo Award）。

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT), working with Prof. Chi (Harold) Liu. I received my Ph.D. in Software Engineering from Dalian University of Technology, and spent time at Peking University on an academic exchange during my doctoral studies.

My research focuses on underwater optical wireless communication, wireless sensing, and wireless rechargeable sensor networks. My work has appeared in MobiCom, UbiComp, ICNP, IEEE TMC and other venues, and received the Best Demo Award at IEEE PerCom 2025.

研究方向Research Interests

水下无线光通信Underwater Optical Wireless Communication

Underwater Optical Wireless Communication

信号传输精确建模、性能制约分析与高效组网，探索速率、距离与可靠性之间的“不可能三角”。Precise channel modeling, performance-bound analysis and efficient networking, exploring the “impossible trinity” among rate, range and reliability.

无线感知Wireless Sensing

Wireless Sensing

基于 WiFi / 毫米波信号的人员存在性检测、呼吸检测等非接触感知技术，并开展产业应用研究。Contact-free sensing with WiFi and mmWave signals — presence detection, respiration monitoring — with industrial deployment.

无线充电传感器网络Wireless Rechargeable Sensor Networks

Wireless Rechargeable Sensor Networks

利用无线能量传输解决物联网的能量瓶颈，研究充电调度、鲁棒性与安全性问题。Using wireless power transfer to break the energy bottleneck of IoT — charging scheduling, robustness and security.

科研交流与合作For Students & Collaborators

If you are interested in underwater communication & networking, wireless sensing, or IoT systems, I'd love to hear from you. Our work combines modeling and theory with hardware prototype design and underwater experiments.

Undergraduates looking for research experience and colleagues interested in collaboration are welcome to email me at 7520260311@bit.com with a CV and a few words about your interests.
